# Masterclass Module: Accessible Washrooms under BP 344 (2024 IRR)

## Module Overview
This module provides a comprehensive guide to designing and implementing accessible washrooms in compliance with the **Building Physical Accessibility (BP 344) 2024 IRR**. It focuses on specific dimensions, safety features, and material requirements to ensure inclusivity for persons with disabilities.

---

## 1. Learning Objectives
By the end of this module, participants will be able to:
*   Identify the minimum clear floor space and turning requirements for accessible stalls.
*   Apply correct dimensions for water closets, lavatories, and urinals.
*   Install grab bars and handrails according to safety standards.
*   Select appropriate materials and finishes for durability and accessibility.
*   Ensure elevator and access point compliance.

---

## 2. Key Standards (BP 344 IRR 2024)
All designs must adhere to the **Building Physical Accessibility (BP 344) 2024 IRR**.
*   **Ratio of Water Closets:** 1 water closet for every 20 occupants, plus 1 additional unit if the total exceeds 20.
*   **Total Water Closets:** 20 total per sex (as per specific project requirements).
*   **Elevator Requirement:** Elevators must meet specific accessibility requirements whenever required.

---

## 3. Detailed Specifications

### 3.1 Water Closet Requirements
*   **Stall Dimensions:** Minimum size of **1.70m x 1.80m** (1700mm x 1800mm).
*   **Seat Height:** **0.45m** (450mm).
*   **Flush Handle Height:** **1.20m** (1200mm).
*   **Door Width:** Minimum **32"** (815mm).
*   **Door Opening:** Can open inward or outward.
*   **Partitions:** Must be floor-mounted, wall-hung, or suspended.
*   **Materials:** Metal (enamel, porcelain, stainless), Plastic laminate, tempered glass, marble.

### 3.2 Lavatory & Sink Requirements
*   **Maximum Height:** **0.80m** (800mm).
*   **Knee Recess Height:** **0.60m - 0.70m** (600mm - 700mm).
*   **Knee Recess Depth:** **0.50m** (500mm).
*   **Specialized Heights:**
    *   Children: **700mm**.
    *   Hospitals: **760mm**.
    *   Most other situations: **785mm - 800mm**.
*   **Button Heights:** **0.90m - 1.20m** (900mm - 1200mm).
*   **Color Contrast:** Accessories must have color contrast for visibility.

### 3.3 Urinal Requirements
*   **Height:** **0.48m** (480mm).

### 3.4 Grab Bars & Safety Features
*   **Minimum Length:** **36"** (915mm) long, centered on the WC.
*   **Extension:** Must extend on the transfer side.
*   **Vertical Bar Position:** **350mm - 450mm** from the front edge.
*   **Distance from Wall:** **750mm** from finish to wall to grab bar.
*   **Handrail Height:** **0.80m** (800mm).
*   **Reachability:** Must ensure usability and accessibility for all users.

### 3.5 Turning Space & Circulation
*   **Turning Space:** Minimum dimension of **1.50m** (1500mm).
*   **Turnabout Dimensions:** **1.50m x 1.50m** (1500mm x 1500mm).
*   **Clear Floor Space:** Minimum dimension of **1.10m x 1.40m** (1100mm x 1400mm).
*   **Elevator Distance:** Distance from entrance **30.00** (Note: Context implies 3000mm or 3m for accessibility).

### 3.6 Door & Access Requirements
*   **Door Opening:** Inward or outward.
*   **From Edge of Door:** **0.20m** (200mm) clearance.
*   **Elevator Distance:** **30.00** (3000mm).

---

## 4. Materials & Finishes
*   **Flooring:** Must be slip-resistant.
*   **Walls:** Durable, easy to clean, and accessible.
*   **Accessories:** Must provide color contrast for visibility.
*   **General:** Materials must be durable, easy to clean, and accessible.

---

## 5. Compliance Checklist
*   [ ] Stall size is 1.70m x 1.80m.
*   [ ] Water closet height is 0.45m.
*   [ ] Lavatory height is within 700mm - 800mm range.
*   [ ] Knee recess is 0.60m - 0.70m deep.
*   [ ] Grab bars are installed at 750mm from wall and 350-450mm from front edge.
*   [ ] Turning space is 1.50m x 1.50m.
*   [ ] Door width is at least 32" (815mm).
*   [ ] Color contrast is provided for accessories.
*   [ ] Elevator distance from entrance is 30.00 (3000mm).

---

## 6. Conclusion
Adhering to these specifications ensures that washrooms are safe, usable, and accessible for all individuals. Compliance with BP 344 IRR 2024 is mandatory for all new constructions and renovations.
