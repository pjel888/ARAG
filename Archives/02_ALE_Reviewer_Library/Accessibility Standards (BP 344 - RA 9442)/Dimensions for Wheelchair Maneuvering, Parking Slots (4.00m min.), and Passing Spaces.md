Based on the provided text and search results, here are the dimensions for Wheelchair Maneuvering, Parking Slots, and Passing Spaces.

### 1. Accessible Parking Slot Dimensions
According to the **2024 Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations (BP 344)**:
*   **Minimum Width:** **4,000 mm (4.00 m)**.
    *   *Note:* The additional width beyond the standard slot ensures the adjacent pathway accommodates wheelchair passage.
*   **Minimum Length:** **5,000 mm**.
*   **Walkway Between Slots:** A clear walkway of **1,200 mm** must be provided between the front ends of parked cars.
*   **Location:** Slots must never be located in ramped or sloping areas.

### 2. Wheelchair Maneuvering & Turning Spaces
Spaces are required to allow for turning around, resting, and making 180° turns.

| Space Type | Minimum Dimensions | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Turnabout Space** | **1,500 mm x 1,500 mm** (ADA) <br> **1,800 mm x 1,800 mm** (BP 344) | Spaced at a maximum of 12 m apart. |
| **180° Turning Circle** | **60" (1,525 mm) Diameter** | Or a T-shaped area within a 60" square with arms 36" (915 mm) wide. |
| **Rest Stop / Turnaround** | **1,800 mm x 1,800 mm** | Must be level and large enough to "park" safely. |
| **Dead-End Corridor** | **1,800 mm x 1,800 mm** | Must be provided within 3,500 mm of every dead-end. |

### 3. Passing Spaces & Corridors
These dimensions ensure safe passage and interaction between wheelchair users.

*   **Corridor Minimum Width:** **1,200 mm** (Shall have no obstruction).
*   **Accessible Route Path Minimum:** **900 mm (36 in)**.
    *   *Context:* Most wheelchairs are 800–900 mm wide.
*   **Passing Space Interval:**
    *   In lengthy or busy pathways, spaces must be provided at periodic intervals.
    *   **Minimum Clear Dimension:** **1,800 mm**.
    *   **Maximum Spacing:** **12,000 mm (12 m)** between rest stops/turnabout spaces.
*   **Doorway Minimum Clear Opening:** **32" (815 mm)**.
*   **Door Swing:** Must not swing into the required clear floor space.

### 4. Wheelchair Dimensions (Reference)
For design context, typical occupied dimensions (excluding children) are:

| Chair Type | Length (Occupied) | Width (Occupied) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Manual Wheelchair** | 850–1,250 mm | 700–1,200 mm |
| **Attendant Propelled** | 1,200–1,570 mm | 800–1,350 mm |
| **Electric Wheelchair** | 860–1,520 mm | 700–1,400 mm |
| **Electric Scooter** | 1,170–1,600 mm | 1,170–1,500 mm |

### Key Design Principles
*   **Closed Networks:** Pedestrian accessible routes should form closed networks rather than discontiguous units.
*   **Level Surfaces:** Rest stops and turning spaces must be level.
*   **Tactile Guidance:** Directional tactile blocks should be installed at free and unobstructed walkways to guide persons with visual disabilities.
