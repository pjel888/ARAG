# MASTERCLASS MODULE: Housing Infrastructure Site Planning
## Topic: Comparison of Road Widths and Open Space (BP 220 vs. PD 957)

### 1. Module Overview
This module analyzes the regulatory differences between **Batas Pambansa 220 (BP 220)** and **Presidential Decree 957 (PD 957)** regarding housing infrastructure site planning. Specifically, we will examine how these decrees dictate **road widths** and **open space requirements**, and how these standards influence the design of economic/socialized housing versus open market housing.

---

### 2. Key Definitions
*   **BP 220:** Governs **Economic and Socialized Housing**. It is designed to address housing needs for lower-income groups, often requiring cost-effective but compliant infrastructure.
*   **PD 957:** Governs **Open Market Housing**. It applies to commercial and higher-value residential developments, typically requiring adherence to stricter or more detailed infrastructure standards (often referencing BP 344).

---

### 3. Comparative Analysis: Road Widths & Open Space

#### A. Road Width Standards
The primary distinction between the two decrees lies in the **level of detail** and **flexibility** regarding road dimensions.

| Feature | BP 220 (Economic/Socialized Housing) | PD 957 (Open Market Housing) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Detail Level** | **High Detail.** Provides specific, detailed requirements for road hierarchies, rights-of-way, widths, slopes, and materials. | **Less Detail.** Provides less detail on requirements that vary by project size. |
| **Compliance Standard** | Internal standards within the decree. | Requires compliance with **BP 344** for road standards. |
| **Project Size Variance** | Requirements are explicitly based on project size. | Less emphasis on varying requirements based on project size. |
| **Infrastructure Goal** | Ensures adequate roads within subdivisions and connections to major transportation routes, prioritizing cost-efficiency. | Ensures adequate roads within subdivisions and connections to major transportation routes, prioritizing market standards. |

#### B. Open Space Requirements
Both decrees mandate that housing developments must conform with zoning ordinances regarding open spaces.

*   **Common Requirement:** Both BP 220 and PD 957 require **densities and open spaces** to conform with zoning ordinances.
*   **Land Requirements:** Both decrees specify land requirements for **non-saleable areas**, such as parks and facilities.
*   **Contextual Importance:**
    *   **Safety & Traffic:** As noted in general infrastructure studies, street width significantly impacts safety. Wider roads (e.g., 7.20m) are often associated with higher accident rates compared to narrower streets (e.g., 5.50m).
    *   **Parking & Speed:** Adequate road width is necessary to accommodate parking provisions and manage traffic speed. Narrower streets (e.g., typical 24 ft / ~7.3m) can help slow traffic and reduce accidents, a consideration that may influence the "detailed requirements" found in BP 220 for socialized housing.

---

### 4. Design Implications

#### For BP 220 Projects (Socialized Housing)
*   **Design Strategy:** Utilize the **detailed requirements** to optimize infrastructure costs.
*   **Road Width:** Adhere to specific width, slope, and material standards provided for the specific project size.
*   **Open Space:** Ensure non-saleable areas (parks) meet the minimum land requirements to satisfy zoning ordinances.

#### For PD 957 Projects (Open Market Housing)
*   **Design Strategy:** Focus on compliance with **BP 344** rather than internal PD 957 variations.
*   **Road Width:** Ensure roads meet the standards of BP 344. While PD 957 provides less detail on project size variations, the underlying BP 344 standards (often stricter for open market) must be met.
*   **Open Space:** Similar to BP 220, ensure conformity with zoning ordinances, but with an eye toward the higher value of the land and facilities.

---

### 5. Summary of Differences
1.  **Specificity:** BP 220 offers **more detailed requirements** for road widths and open spaces based on project size. PD 957 offers **less detail** and defers to BP 344 for road standards.
2.  **Housing Type:** BP 220 is for **economic/socialized housing**; PD 957 is for **open market housing**.
3.  **Compliance:** PD 957 explicitly requires road standards to comply with **BP 344**, whereas BP 220 has its own detailed specifications.

### 6. Conclusion
When planning housing infrastructure, developers must distinguish between the two decrees. **BP 220** allows for a more granular approach to road width and open space design based on the specific scale of the project, which is beneficial for cost management in socialized housing. **PD 957**, while covering similar ground (rights-of-way, hierarchies), relies on the broader **BP 344** standards and provides less flexibility regarding project size variations. Understanding these nuances ensures that the road width and open space design not only meets legal requirements but also optimizes safety and functionality for the specific housing market segment.
