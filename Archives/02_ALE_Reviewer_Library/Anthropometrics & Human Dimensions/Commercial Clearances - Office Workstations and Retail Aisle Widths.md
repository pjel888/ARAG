# Masterclass Module: Commercial Clearances
## Office Workstations and Retail Aisle Widths

### **Module Overview**
This module provides technical standards for planning commercial spaces, specifically focusing on **Retail Shops** and **General Offices**. It covers minimum aisle widths, workstation clearances, and storage dimensions necessary for safe circulation and functional efficiency.

**Learning Objectives:**
*   Identify minimum and recommended aisle widths for retail environments.
*   Determine circulation requirements for office workstations and main exits.
*   Understand space allocation for secretarial and clerical areas.
*   Differentiate between physical clearance standards and legal commercial regulations.

---

### **Section 1: Retail Shop Clearances (Fig. 2)**
*Focus: Customer flow, product accessibility, and checkout efficiency.*

In retail environments, aisle widths are critical for customer safety and product display. The following standards apply to general retail shops (excluding grocery-specific requirements).

| Aisle Type | Minimum Width | Recommended Width | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clerk Aisles** | 1'-8" | 2'-0" to 2'-3" | Narrow aisles for staff access only. |
| **Clerk Aisles (Grocery)** | N/A | 3'-0" | Wider clearance required for grocery carts. |
| **Public Aisles (Main)** | 4'-6" | 5'-6" to 7'-0" | Primary circulation paths for customers. |
| **Public Aisles (Max)** | N/A | Up to 11'-0" | Used for large display areas or wide open spaces. |
| **Public Aisles (Secondary)** | 3'-0" | 3'-6" | Secondary circulation paths. |

**Key Consideration:**
*   **Work Station Space:** Space assigned to a work station must include the furniture, equipment, and necessary circulation area.
*   **Clerk Aisles:** These are typically narrower (1'-8" min) to maximize display space, but must allow staff to move safely.

### **Section 2: Office Planning Clearances (Fig. 7, Fig. 6, Fig. 1, Fig. 2)**
*Focus: Employee safety, workflow efficiency, and ergonomic movement.*

Office planning requires specific aisle widths to ensure employees can move freely between workstations and exits without obstruction.

#### **A. Aisle Width Standards**
| Aisle Category | Width Requirement | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Aisles** | **60 inches** (5'-0") | Aisles leading directly to main exits. |
| **Intermediate Aisles** | **48 inches** (4'-0") | Connecting aisles between work zones. |
| **Secondary Aisles** | **36 inches** (3'-0") | Aisles between rows of desks. |

#### **B. Workstation Space Allocation**
*   **Secretarial/Clerical Areas:**
    *   **Standard:** 45 to 60 sq. ft. per person (including aisle space).
    *   **Side Chairs:** Add an additional **10 to 15 sq. ft.** per person for side chairs.
*   **Private Offices:**
    *   Must include clearances for the desk, bookcase, and passage.
    *   Standard configurations include: Desk + Bookcase + Passage / Desk + Passage / Typist + Passage.

#### **C. Storage and Library Shelving**
*   **Clearances:** Similar aisle clearances apply to storage and library shelving areas.
*   **File Storage:** Ensure aisles allow for the retrieval of files without blocking circulation paths.

### **Section 3: Regulatory & Legal Context (Search Results)**
*Focus: Understanding the distinction between physical standards and legal compliance.*

While physical clearances are technical requirements, commercial spaces are also subject to legal frameworks.

*   **National Building Code (Philippines - DPWH):**
    *   Minimum sizes of courts and spaces are governed by use, construction, and height.
    *   *Note:* Physical clearances (like the 36" or 60" office aisles) must align with building codes regarding egress and safety.
*   **Commercial Rent & Lease Terms:**
    *   Commercial rent increases are generally not covered by rent control laws.
    *   *Relevance to Planning:* When designing commercial spaces, ensure that the physical layout (clearances) does not violate lease terms or building codes, even if financial regulations differ.

---

### **Section 4: Summary & Key Takeaways**

1.  **Retail vs. Office:** Retail aisles prioritize customer flow (Main: 4'-6" min), while office aisles prioritize employee safety and workflow (Main: 60" min).
2.  **Minimums vs. Recommendations:** Always adhere to the *Minimum* width for code compliance, but aim for the *Recommended* width for better user experience.
3.  **Space Calculation:** When planning office space, remember to add 10-15 sq. ft. per person for side chairs in addition to the base 45-60 sq. ft.
4.  **Code Compliance:** Physical clearances are distinct from financial lease terms but must still meet building code requirements for safety and egress.
