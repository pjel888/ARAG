# Masterclass Module: Calculating GFA, TGFA, PSO, and Building Height Limits (BHL)
**Based on the National Building Code of the Philippines (PD 1096) & IRR (2008 Annotations)**

---

## 1. Introduction
This module provides a comprehensive guide to calculating the fundamental **Development Controls (DCs)** required for building design and approval in the Philippines. These calculations ensure that proposed structures comply with zoning ordinances, safety standards, and bulk regulations defined by the Professional Regulatory Board of Architecture (PRBOA).

**Key Concepts:**
*   **GFA (Gross Floor Area):** The total floor area of the building.
*   **TGFA (Total Gross Floor Area):** GFA plus specific non-GFA areas (e.g., balconies, pools).
*   **PSO (Percentage of Site Occupancy):** The ratio of the building footprint to the total lot area.
*   **BHL (Building Height Limit):** The maximum allowable height for a structure.

---

## 2. Key Definitions

| Acronym | Full Name | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **GFA** | Gross Floor Area | The total floor space within the main and auxiliary buildings. |
| **TGFA** | Total Gross Floor Area | GFA + All other enclosed support areas + Usable horizontal areas (above/below grade) physically attached to the building. |
| **PSO** | Percentage of Site Occupancy | The ratio of the Allowable Maximum Building Footprint (AMBF) to the Total Lot Area (TLA). |
| **BHL** | Building Height Limit | The maximum height allowed for a building based on zoning and lot characteristics. |
| **AMBF** | Allowable Maximum Building Footprint | The maximum horizontal area the building can occupy on the lot. |
| **OFB** | Outermost Faces of Building | The vertical projections of the outermost faces of the AMBF up to the BHL. |
| **Non-GFA** | Excluded Floor Areas | Areas like helipads, AC cooling towers, water tanks, pools, terraces, gardens, and balconies exceeding 10.00 sq. meters. |

---

## 3. The Formulas

### A. Calculating Total Gross Floor Area (TGFA)
To approximate building volume, you must convert GFA to TGFA using the specific multiplier based on the building's occupancy type.

$$TGFA = GFA \times \text{Multiplier}$$

*Alternatively, using the additive method:*
$$TGFA = GFA + \text{Non-GFA Areas}$$

### B. Calculating Percentage of Site Occupancy (PSO)
$$PSO = \frac{\text{Allowable Maximum Building Footprint (AMBF)}}{\text{Total Lot Area (TLA)}} \times 100\%$$

### C. Calculating Resultant Building Height
$$\text{Resultant Height} = \text{BHL (in floors)} \times \text{Desired Floor-to-Floor Height}$$

## 5. Step-by-Step Calculation Guide

Follow this workflow to ensure compliance with PD 1096:

### Step 1: Determine the Building Type
Identify the occupancy type (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, etc.) to select the correct **Multiplier** from Table VII.G.2.

### Step 2: Calculate GFA
Sum up all enclosed floor areas within the main and auxiliary buildings.
*   *Note:* Do not include helipads, AC cooling towers, water tanks, roof decks, laundry, pools, terraces, gardens, balconies > 10 sqm, fire escapes, etc.

### Step 3: Calculate TGFA
Apply the multiplier to your GFA.
*   *Example:* If GFA is 100 sqm and the building is Residential 1 (Multiplier 1.50), then TGFA = 100 * 1.50 = 150 sqm.

### Step 4: Calculate PSO
Determine the footprint of the building (AMBF) and divide it by the Total Lot Area (TLA).
*   *Check:* Ensure PSO does not exceed the allowable limit for the specific zone (e.g., 40%, 50%, 60%, etc.).

### Step 5: Calculate Resultant Height
Multiply the Building Height Limit (BHL) in floors by the actual floor-to-floor height.
*   *Check:* Ensure the Resultant Height does not exceed the BHL. If it does, you must seek relief or reduce the height.

---

## 6. Important Considerations & Constraints

*   **Setbacks:** Ensure the building footprint respects the required front, side, and rear setbacks.
*   **Relief Options:** If the calculated height exceeds the BHL, you may apply for relief from the Local Government Unit (LGU) or reduce the floor-to-floor height.
*   **Sub-Grade Solutions:** Consider using sub-grade solutions (e.g., parking below ground) to reduce the above-grade height and footprint.
*   **Volume Calculation:** Remember that TGFA is used for volume calculations, not just GFA.
*   **Non-GFA Areas:** Be careful with balconies. If a balcony exceeds 10.00 sq. meters, it is considered a Non-GFA area and must be included in the TGFA calculation.

## 7. Practice Scenario

**Scenario:**
You are designing a **Commercial 2** building on a lot with a **Total Lot Area (TLA)** of 500 sqm.
*   **GFA** calculated: 200 sqm.
*   **Floor-to-Floor Height:** 3.5 meters.
*   **BHL:** 4 floors.

**Calculations:**
1.  **Multiplier:** Commercial 2 = **1.33**.
2.  **TGFA:** $200 \times 1.33 = 266 \text{ sqm}$.
3.  **PSO:** Assume AMBF is 250 sqm. $PSO = (250 / 500) \times 100\% = 50\%$.
4.  **Resultant Height:** $4 \text{ floors} \times 3.5 \text{ meters} = 14 \text{ meters}$.

**Check:** Does 14 meters exceed the BHL? If the BHL is defined as 14 meters, you are compliant. If the BHL is 12 meters, you must reduce the floor-to-floor height or seek relief.
