# MASTERCLASS MODULE: PD 1096 Rule VIII – Light and Ventilation
**Focus:** Window Sizes, Room Dimensions, and Court Requirements

---

## 🎯 Module Objective
To understand the minimum standards for light, ventilation, room sizes, and court dimensions as mandated by the **National Building Code of the Philippines (PD 1096) Rule VIII**.

---

## 1. Section 801: General Requirements of Light and Ventilation

Every building must be designed, constructed, and equipped to provide adequate light and ventilation.

### Key Provisions:
*   **Civil Code Compliance:** Must adhere to the Civil Code of the Philippines regarding easements of light and view.
*   **Street Facing:** All buildings must face a street, public alley, or an approved private street.
*   **No Reduction of Standards:**
    *   No building shall be altered to reduce the size of any room.
    *   No building shall be altered to reduce the relative area of windows below the Code's requirements.
    *   **Exception:** An additional room may be created *only if* it conforms to the requirements of this Rule.
*   **No Reduction of Open Space:** No building shall be enlarged if it reduces the required court or yard dimensions below the prescribed limits.

---

## 2. Minimum Sizes and Dimensions of Rooms

Per the **Guidelines on Easements, View Corridors/Sight Lines and Basements**, the following minimum sizes apply to Group A dwellings:

| Room Type | Minimum Area | Minimum Least Horizontal Dimension |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Human Habitation** | **6.00 sq.m.** | **2.00 m** |
| **Kitchen** | **3.00 sq.m.** | **1.50 m** |
| **Bath and Toilet** | **1.20 sq.m.** | **900 mm** |

> **Note:** These dimensions ensure adequate space for occupancy and movement.

---

## 3. Court Dimensions and Site Occupancy (Section 802)

Courts (open spaces) are required to provide light and ventilation. The width of the court depends on the height of the building.

### Width Requirements:
*   **1 to 2 Storeys:** Minimum width of **2.00 meters**.
*   **Quadruplexes:** May be reduced to **1.50 meters**.
*   **3 to 14 Storeys:** The minimum width must be increased by **300 mm (0.30 m)** for each additional storey above two.
    *   *Formula:* `2.00 m + (0.30 m × Number of additional storeys)`
    *   *Example:* A 3-storey building requires a court width of `2.00 + 0.30 = 2.30 m`.

### Measurement of Site Occupancy:
*   Courts and yards are measured from the **outer face of the wall**.
*   This ensures that the open space is not encroached upon by the building's footprint.

---

## 4. Window Openings (Section 808)

While specific area ratios (e.g., 1:2) are detailed in **Section 808**, the general rule is that window openings must be sufficient to provide the required light and ventilation.

*   **Requirement:** Window openings must be provided in accordance with Section 808.
*   **Purpose:** To ensure cross-ventilation and natural lighting for the rooms defined in Section 801.

---

## 5. Fire Safety and Exit Courts

In addition to light and ventilation, courts must also serve as fire safety measures.

*   **Exit Courts:** Every building must have an exit court or yard.
*   **Openings:** Openings in the walls of the court must be provided to allow for the escape of smoke and heat.
*   **Fire Escape:** The court must be accessible for fire escape purposes.

---

## 📝 Module Summary & Key Takeaways

1.  **Habitation Room:** Must be at least **6 sq.m.** with a width of **2m**.
2.  **Kitchen:** Must be at least **3 sq.m.** with a width of **1.5m**.
3.  **Bath/Toilet:** Must be at least **1.2 sq.m.** with a width of **900mm**.
4.  **Court Width (1-2 Storeys):** Minimum **2m**.
5.  **Court Width (3+ Storeys):** Increases by **300mm** per storey.
6.  **Prohibition:** Do not alter a building to reduce room sizes or window areas below the Code.
