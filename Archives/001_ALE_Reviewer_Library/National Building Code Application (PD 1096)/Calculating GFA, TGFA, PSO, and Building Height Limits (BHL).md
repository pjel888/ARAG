## 📚 Section 1: Key Definitions

Before calculating, you must understand the terminology defined in the Guidelines on Building Bulk and Development Controls.

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GFA (Gross Floor Area)** | The total floor space within the main and auxiliary buildings. It includes all enclosed support areas and usable horizontal surfaces above and below established grade level physically attached to the building. |
| **TGFA (Total Gross Floor Area)** | The total floor space consisting of **GFA** + **Non-GFA areas** (e.g., balconies, pools, terraces). It approximates the building volume. |
| **AMBF (Allowable Maximum Building Footprint)** | The maximum horizontal area of the building footprint allowed on the lot. |
| **BHL (Building Height Limit)** | The maximum height allowed for a building/structure based on zoning ordinances. |
| **PSO (Percentage of Site Occupancy)** | The ratio of the AMBF to the Total Lot Area (TLA). |
| **Non-GFA Areas** | Uncovered areas such as helipads, ACCU balconies, overhead water tanks, roof decks, laundry areas, cages, pools, whirlpools, terraces, gardens, courts, plazas, and balconies exceeding 10.00 sq. meters. |

---

## 🧮 Section 2: The Formulas

### 1. Calculating TGFA from GFA
To convert GFA to TGFA, you must add the Non-GFA areas. The most efficient way is to use the **Multiplier** based on the building type.

$$TGFA = GFA \times \text{Multiplier}$$

*Alternatively:*
$$TGFA = GFA + \text{Non-GFA Areas}$$

### 2. Calculating Percentage of Site Occupancy (PSO)
$$PSO = \frac{\text{Allowable Maximum Building Footprint (AMBF)}}{\text{Total Lot Area (TLA)}}$$

### 3. Calculating Resultant Building Height
$$\text{Resultant Height} = \text{BHL (in floors)} \times \text{Desired Floor-to-Floor Height}$$

## 🛠️ Section 4: Step-by-Step Calculation Guide

Follow this workflow to ensure compliance with the National Building Code:

1.  **Determine GFA:** Calculate the enclosed floor area of the building.
2.  **Identify Non-GFA Areas:** List all excluded areas (balconies, pools, terraces >10sqm, etc.).
3.  **Determine Building Type:** Identify the occupancy type (e.g., Residential 1, Commercial 2).
4.  **Find Multiplier:** Look up the corresponding multiplier in **Table VII.G.2**.
5.  **Calculate TGFA:** Multiply GFA by the Multiplier.
6.  **Calculate AMBF:** Determine the allowable footprint based on local zoning ordinances.
7.  **Calculate PSO:** Divide AMBF by Total Lot Area (TLA).
8.  **Check Height:** Multiply the number of floors by the floor-to-floor height to ensure it does not exceed the BHL.

## 📝 Section 5: Example Problem

**Scenario:**
You are designing a **Residential 1** building.
*   **Total Lot Area (TLA):** 500 sq. meters
*   **Enclosed GFA:** 300 sq. meters
*   **Balconies & Terraces (Non-GFA):** 150 sq. meters
*   **Building Height Limit (BHL):** 10 Floors
*   **Floor-to-Floor Height:** 3.5 meters

**Step 1: Calculate TGFA**
*   *Method A (Using Multiplier):*
    *   Building Type: Residential 1
    *   Multiplier: **1.50**
    *   $TGFA = 300 \text{ sqm} \times 1.50 = \mathbf{450 \text{ sqm}}$
*   *Method B (Summation):*
    *   $TGFA = GFA + \text{Non-GFA}$
    *   $TGFA = 300 + 150 = 450 \text{ sqm}$

**Step 2: Calculate PSO**
*   *Assumption:* The AMBF is set at 300 sqm (60% of TLA).
*   $PSO = \frac{300}{500} = \mathbf{60\%}$

**Step 3: Calculate Resultant Height**
*   $Height = 10 \text{ floors} \times 3.5 \text{ meters} = \mathbf{35 \text{ meters}}$

**Result:** The building complies with the TGFA and Height Limits.

## ⚠️ Section 6: Important Considerations & Notes

*   **Exclusions:** Remember that helipads, ACCU balconies, overhead water tanks, roof decks, laundry areas, cages, pools, whirlpools, terraces, gardens, courts, plazas, and balconies exceeding 10.00 sq. meters are **excluded** from GFA.
*   **Relief Options:** If the calculated TGFA or PSO exceeds the allowable limit, you may need to apply for relief options or variances from the Local Building Official (LBO).
*   **Vertical Projections:** The resultant height is determined by the vertical projections of the building.
*   **Local Ordinances:** Always check with your Local Government Unit (LGU) for specific zoning ordinances that may modify the BHL or PSO limits.
