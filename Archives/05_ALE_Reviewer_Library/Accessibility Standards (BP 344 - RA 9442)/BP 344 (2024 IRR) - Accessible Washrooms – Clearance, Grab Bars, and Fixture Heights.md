## Core Concept

**Historical/Legal Context:** BP 344 (Accessibility Law) 2024 IRR updates align with international standards for PWD access in public buildings, integrated with PD 1096.[web:12]

**Definitions:**

- **Accessible Washroom:** Toilet facility for wheelchair users.
- **Clear Floor Space:** Unobstructed area for maneuvering.
- **Grab Bars:** Support bars near fixtures.
- **Turning Space:** 180° wheelchair turn area.[web:17]

**Specific Dimensions (2024 IRR):**

- Accessible water closet **stall area**: **1.70 m × 1.80 m**
- Turning space outside stall: **2.25 m²** (min 1.50 m dimension)
- Toilet seat height: **450 mm** (0.45 m)
- Lavatory height: **800 mm** (0.80 m); knee clearance ≥650 mm
- Flush control: ≤**1.20 m**
- Grab bars: **680-850 mm** above floor
- Door width: **minimum 800 mm**
- Signage: Gender symbols (triangle men, circle women), **300 mm**, ≥7.5 mm thick [web:12][web:16][web:17]

**Analogies:**

1. **Stall as parking space:** 1.70×1.80 m fits wheelchair precisely.
2. **Grab bars as handrails:** Support during transfers.
3. **Turning space as roundabout:** Enables full maneuver.[web:17]

**Practical Design Implications:** In dense Manila areas like Quezon City, compact 1.70×1.80 m stalls maximize space while meeting PWD needs.[web:12]

## Common Mistakes

1. **Square stall assumption:** Use 1.70×1.80 m, not 1.5×1.5 m.
2. **Missing turning space:** Required outside stall (2.25 m²).
3. **Wrong grab bar height:** 680-850 mm range.
4. **Fixture heights:** Toilet 450 mm, lavatory 800 mm.
5. **Narrow doors:** Minimum 800 mm.[web:12][web:17]
