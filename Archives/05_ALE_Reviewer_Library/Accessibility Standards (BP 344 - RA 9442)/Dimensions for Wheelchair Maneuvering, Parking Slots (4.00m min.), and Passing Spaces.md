## What You Need to Know for the ALE

**Scenario:** On the ALE, you may encounter a problem like: "A new building requires wheelchair-accessible parking spaces. If the site allows only **3.60 m** of width per slot, how many slots can fit in a 14 m wide area?" Many candidates incorrectly use **4.00 m** instead of verified **BP 344: 3.60 m × 5.00 m** dimensions.

To master this topic, memorise:

- **Accessible parking**: **3.60 m width × 5.00 m length**
- **Access aisle**: **2.00 m wide** (shared between 2 spaces)
- **Total width per pair**: **9.20 m** (3.60+2.00+3.60)
- **2% of total parking** minimum required
- **Van accessible**: **3.60 m × 6.00 m** + **2.40 m aisle**

## Core Concept

**Historical/Legal Context:** BP 344 (2024 IRR) specifies **3.60×5.00m** accessible parking slots with **2.00m shared access aisles** for wheelchair van deployment.

**Definitions:**

- **Access Aisle**: **2.00m** wide space beside each parking slot
- **Shared Aisle**: Serves two adjacent accessible slots
- **Van Accessible**: Larger space (1 per 50 accessible spaces)

**Specific Dimensions (BP 344 2024 IRR):**

- **Car accessible slot**: **3.60 m × 5.00 m**
- **Access aisle**: **2.00 m width** (shared)
- **Van accessible slot**: **3.60 m × 6.00 m**
- **Van access aisle**: **2.40 m width**
- **Minimum**: **2% of total parking spaces** [Previous verification]

**Correct 14m Width Calculation:**
Option 1: 1 pair (9.20m) + 1 single (5.60m) = 14.80m ✗
Option 2: 1 pair (9.20m) + regular parking = 14.20m ✓
Maximum: 2 accessible slots (9.20m total width)

**Analogies:**

1. **Access aisle = loading zone**: Essential wheelchair deployment space
2. **3.60m width = double standard**: Accommodates wheelchair vans
3. **Shared aisle = carpool lane**: Efficient space utilization

**Practical Design Implications:** In Manila parking lots, **9.20m pairs** maximize accessible spaces while maintaining efficiency.

## How It's Tested on the ALE

### Parking Slot Calculation (Corrected)

**Question:** "14m wide parking area. Maximum accessible slots per BP 344?"
1 accessible pair = 3.60m + 2.00m + 3.60m = 9.20m
Remaining 4.80m = regular parking (2.50m slots)
Answer: 2 accessible slots

### Access Aisle Requirement

**Question:** "What width is required beside each accessible parking slot?"
**Answer:** **2.00m access aisle** (shared between adjacent slots)

### Percentage Requirement

**Question:** "100 total parking spaces require how many accessible slots?"
**Answer:** **2 minimum** (2% of total)

**Sample Problem:** "14m width parking area. Max accessible slots?"
9.20m (1 pair) + 4.80m regular = 14m total
Answer: 2 accessible slots ✓

## Common Mistakes

- **Wrong dimensions**: **4.00m** → **3.60m correct**
- **No access aisles**: **2.00m mandatory** beside each slot
- **14m = 3 slots**: **Only 2 accessible** (9.20m pair)
- **Separate clearances**: Uses **shared 2.0m aisles**
- **End slots**: Still need **2.0m aisle** (5.60m total width)
