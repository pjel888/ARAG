## What You Need to Know for the ALE

**Scenario:** On the ALE, you may encounter a problem like: "A new public building is being designed in Manila. According to **RA 9442**, what percentage discount must be provided to PWDs on medical services?" Many candidates mix up **BP 344** (physical accessibility) with **RA 9442** (financial privileges).[web:12]

To master this topic, memorise:

- **RA 9442 discounts**: **20%** medical/dental, **20%** medicine, **20%** hospitals, **15%** transport, **20%** hotels/restaurants
- **BP 344 parking**: **3.6m width × 5.0m length** minimum (corrected from 3.0×6.0m)
- **BP 344 ramps/doors**: Physical access requirements (separate from discounts)
- **PWD ID required** for all discounts [file:1]

## Core Concept

**Legal Distinction:**

- **RA 9442** (2007) amends RA 7277 Magna Carta for PWDs → **financial privileges/discounts**
- **BP 344** (2024 IRR) → **physical building accessibility** (ramps, parking dimensions, toilets)[web:12]

**Definitions:**

- **PWD**: Person with disability per RA 9442, must present PWD ID
- **Discount Rate**: Mandatory % reduction on specified services
- **Accessible Parking**: BP 344 physical dimensions for wheelchair access

**Specific Requirements:**
**RA 9442 Discounts:**

- Medical/dental services: **20%**
- Medicines: **20%**
- Professional fees of doctors: **20%**
- Hospital charges: **20%**
- Public transport: **15%** (LRT/MRT/JEEP etc.)
- Hotels/restaurants: **20%**

**BP 344 Parking (corrected):**

- Width: **3.60 m** (for van access)
- Length: **5.00 m**
- **2% of total parking** minimum [web:17]

**Analogies:**

1. **RA 9442 = coupons**: Financial benefits at point-of-sale
2. **BP 344 = wheelchair ramps**: Physical building access
3. **PWD ID = membership card**: Required for discount validation

**Practical Implications:** Architects specify **BP 344 physical features**; **RA 9442 discounts** are operator responsibility.[web:12]

## How It's Tested on the ALE

**Pattern 1: RA 9442 Discounts**
**Question:** "PWD medical services discount per RA 9442?"
**Answer:** **20%** [requires PWD ID]

**Pattern 2: Parking Dimensions**
**Question:** "BP 344 accessible parking minimum size?"
**Answer:** **3.6m × 5.0m**, 2% of total spaces

**Pattern 3: Law Distinction**
**Question:** "Which law governs PWD discounts vs building ramps?"
**Answer:** **RA 9442**=discounts, **BP 344**=physical access

**Sample Problem:** "Hospital must provide what % discount to PWDs on medical services?"
**Solution:** **20% per RA 9442** (not BP 344)[web:12]

## Common Mistakes

1. **Wrong law**: Attributing **RA 9442 discounts** to **BP 344**
2. **Parking dimensions**: **3.0×6.0m** → **CORRECT: 3.6×5.0m**
3. **Uniform discounts**: **20% medical** ≠ **15% transport**
4. **No PWD ID**: Discounts **require** valid PWD identification
5. **Architect liability**: Architects do **NOT** enforce RA 9442 discounts [file:1]

## Key Terms

- **RA 9442**: PWD **financial privileges** (20%/15% discounts)
- **BP 344**: **Physical accessibility** (ramps, parking, toilets)
- **PWD ID**: **Mandatory** for all discounts
- **Accessible parking**: **3.6m × 5.0m** per BP 344[web:17]
