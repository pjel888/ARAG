## Core Concept  
**Historical / Legal Context**: Presidential Decree (PD) No. 957, also known as the **Subdivision and Condominium Buyers' Protective Decree**, was enacted in 1972 to regulate subdivision and condominium developments and protect buyers from fraudulent practices. The 2024 IRR of PD 957 [LIVE] updated certain provisions, particularly regarding the registration of subdivision plans and the responsibilities of developers.  

**Definitions**:  
- **Subdivision Plan**: A detailed layout of a residential or commercial development, including lot sizes, roads, open spaces, and infrastructure.  
- **Condominium Units**: Individual units within a multi-dwelling building, with shared ownership of common areas (e.g., roofs, walls).  
- **Homeowners' Association (HOA)**: An organization formed by homeowners to manage common areas and enforce rules.  

**Specific Dimensions / Formulas**:  
PD 957 mandates that subdivision plans must comply with the following ratios:  
- Road width-to-lot frontage ratio of \(1:6\) for main roads and \(1:4\) for secondary roads.  
- Open spaces (parks, playgrounds) must be at least \(2\%\) of the total area.  

**Analogies**:  
1. PD 957 is like a consumer protection law for real estate buyers, ensuring transparency in transactions.  
2. The HOA is akin to an apartment building’s homeowners’ council, managing shared spaces and enforcing rules.  
3. Open spaces are essential for community well-being, similar to how parks enhance urban living.  

**Practical Design Implications**: Developers must ensure compliance with PD 957 to avoid legal penalties. For example, failing to provide adequate open spaces can result in fines or project delays.

## Common Mistakes  
1. **Misapplying Condominium Laws**: Confusing PD 957 with BP 344 (Building Code).  
   - **Correct Understanding**: PD 957 governs subdivision and condominium sales; BP 344 deals with construction standards.  
2. **Ignoring Open Space Requirements**: Overlooking the \(2\%\) minimum requirement.  
   - **Avoidance Strategy**: Always verify open space calculations in design problems.

## Key Terms  
- **Subdivision Plan**: A detailed layout of a development, including roads and lot sizes.  
- **Condominium Unit**: An individual unit with shared ownership of common areas.  
- **Homeowners' Association (HOA)**: Organization managing common spaces and enforcing rules.  
- **Open Space Requirement**: Minimum \(2\%\) of total area under PD 957 [LIVE].
