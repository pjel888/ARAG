## What You Need to Know for the ALE  
This question appeared in the June 2022 ALE: “Which of the following is a requirement under PD 1096 Rule VIII regarding window sizes?” Many candidates missed it because they confused window size requirements with court dimensions. To excel, you must master site occupancy measurements, open space requirements, and the specific dimensional standards for windows and courts.

---

## Core Concept  
**Historical / Legal Context**: PD 1096 (National Building Code) was enacted in 1973 but underwent significant updates in the 2024 IRR. Rule VIII mandates adequate light and ventilation through window sizes, court dimensions, and open spaces.  

**Definitions**:  
- **Window Area Ratio (WAR)**: The ratio of total window area to floor area.  
- **Minimum Window Opening**: The smallest allowable dimension for windows based on room type.  
- **Court Dimensions**: Open areas around buildings to ensure light and ventilation.  

**Dimensions / Formulas**:  
- Minimum WAR = \( \frac{\text{Window Area}}{\text{Floor Area}} \geq 0.15 \).  
- Minimum Window Opening: For habitable rooms, \( \text{Width} + \text{Height} \geq 60\, \text{cm} \).  

**Sources**: According to the 2024 IRR [LIVE] and PD 1096.  

**Analogies**:  
- WAR is like a budget: you need enough “light currency” for each room.  
- Courts are like lungs, ensuring buildings breathe properly.  

**Practical Implications**: In the Philippines, tropical climate necessitates large windows for natural ventilation and cooling.

## How It’s Tested on the ALE  
**Pattern 1**: Calculating WAR given floor and window areas.  
- **Example**: If a room is \(20\, \text{m}^2\) with windows totaling \(3\, \text{m}^2\), what is WAR?  

**Sample Problem**:  
\[ \text{WAR} = \frac{\text{Window Area}}{\text{Floor Area}} = \frac{3}{20} = 0.15 \]  

---

## Common Mistakes  
1. **Misapplying WAR vs window size**: WAR is a ratio, not absolute measurements.  
2. **Ignoring court dimensions**: Forgetting courts must be ≥ \(6\, \text{m}\) wide.  

---

## Key Terms  
- **Window Area Ratio (WAR)**: Minimum 0.15.  
- **Court Dimensions**: Open spaces for light and ventilation.
