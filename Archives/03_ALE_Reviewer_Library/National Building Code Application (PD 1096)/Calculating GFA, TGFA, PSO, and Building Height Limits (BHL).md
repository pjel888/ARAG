- The distinction between **Gross Floor Area (GFA)** and **Total Gross Floor Area (TGFA)**.

## Core Concept  
### Historical / Legal Context  
The **Building Code of the Philippines** (BP 344) governs land use planning and building regulations. The 2024 IRR updates introduced stricter guidelines on floor area ratios, height limits, and site occupancy percentages to address urban density challenges in the Philippines.  

### Definitions  
- **Gross Floor Area (GFA):** Total usable interior space of a building, excluding non-functional areas like parking and uncovered balconies over 10 sq.m.  
- **Total Gross Floor Area (TGFA):** Includes all functional and non-functional spaces except those explicitly excluded by law (e.g., helipads, cooling towers).  
- **Percentage of Site Occupancy (PSO):** Ratio of GFA to TLA (\( \text{PSO} = \frac{\text{GFA}}{\text{TLA}} \times 100 \% \)).  
- **Building Height Limit (BHL):** Maximum height allowed, determined by FLAR and PSO.  

### Formulas & Dimensions  
- \( \text{Max GFA} = \text{FLAR} \times \text{TLA} \)  
- \( \text{PSO} = \frac{\text{GFA}}{\text{TLA}} \times 100 \% \)  
- BHL is tied to PSO and FLAR, with higher ratios allowing taller buildings.  

### Analogies  
- **PSO:** Like a pie chart showing how much of the site is developed.  
- **FLAR:** Similar to zoning laws in other countries, dictating density limits.  
- **BHL:** Comparable to height restrictions in historical city centers (e.g., Venice’s canals).  

### Practical Implications  
Builders must balance FLAR, PSO, and BHL to maximize profitability while adhering to legal constraints. Real-world challenges include navigating conflicting local ordinances and ensuring compliance with environmental regulations.

## How It’s Tested on the ALE  
### Pattern 1: Direct Calculation  
**Question Style:** “Given TLA=800 sq.m and FLAR=9.0, compute Max GFA.”  
**Concept Tested:** Understanding of \( \text{GFA} = \text{FLAR} \times \text{TLA} \).  
**Example Strategy:** Plug values into the formula directly.  

### Pattern 2: PSO Application  
**Question Style:** “If TLA=1,000 sq.m and GFA=7,500 sq.m, compute PSO.”  
**Concept Tested:** Ability to calculate \( \text{PSO} = \frac{\text{GFA}}{\text{TLA}} \times 100 \% \).  

### Pattern 3: BHL Determination  
**Question Style:** “Determine the BHL for a building with GFA=9,000 sq.m and Max Allowed Area per Floor=600 sq.m.”  
**Concept Tested:** Applying \( \text{BHL} = \frac{\text{GFA}}{\text{Max Allowed Area per Floor}} \).  

### Pattern 4: FLAR vs PSO Conflict  
**Question Style:** “A developer seeks to maximize GFA on a C-3 lot. Explain the trade-offs between FLAR and PSO.”  
**Concept Tested:** Balancing density and height limits under BP 344.

## Common Mistakes  
1. **Misapplying FLAR vs PSO:** Confusing which ratio dictates building height or floor area.  
2. **Ignoring 2024 IRR Updates:** Using outdated ratios instead of the latest guidelines.  
3. **Overlooking Balcony Limits:** Failing to deduct balcony areas from GFA/TGFA.  
4. **Miscalculating PSO:** Forgetting to convert percentages in formulas.  

---

## Key Terms  
- **FLAR (Floor Area Ratio):** Maximum allowable ratio of GFA to TLA.  
- **PSO (Percentage of Site Occupancy):** Ratio of GFA to TLA, expressed as a percentage.  
- **BHL (Building Height Limit):** Maximum height allowed based on FLAR and PSO.  
- **GFA (Gross Floor Area):** Usable interior space excluding non-functional areas.  
- **TGFA (Total Gross Floor Area):** Includes all spaces except explicitly excluded ones.
