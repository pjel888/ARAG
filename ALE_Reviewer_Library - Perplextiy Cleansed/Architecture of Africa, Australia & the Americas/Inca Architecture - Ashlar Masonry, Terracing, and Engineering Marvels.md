## Core Concepts (I-A-T Mnemonic – Chant 3x Now)

**I-A-T**: **I**nca ashlar, **A**dapted terracing, **T**echnical marvels. PH link: Flex walls for quakes, terraces for erosion.

- **Ashlar Masonry**: Polygonal stones precisely cut/fitted dry (no mortar), interlocking via friction (e.g., Machu Picchu, Cusco walls up to 100+ tons/stone).
- **Terracing (Andenes)**: Stepped stone-faced platforms on Andes slopes (up to 45°+); integrated drainage/aqueducts for agriculture, urban stability, landslide prevention.
- **Engineering Marvels**: Aqueducts (e.g., Tipón: 13km, 1-2% gradient, self-cleaning); trapezoidal batter (doors/walls flex in quakes); Qhapaq Ñan road network (40,000km).

**Fact-Check**: Inca Empire 1438-1533 AD; no wheels/iron tools; stones dressed with harder stones (UNESCO confirmed).
