## Core Concepts (P-P-A Mnemonic – Chant Now)

**P-P-A**: **P**yramids (sacred axes), **P**lazas (ceremonial hearts), **A**ztec urbanism (grid on water).

- **Pyramids**: Stepped temple platforms (talud-tablero style), e.g., Teotihuacan Sun (65m, 200 CE)/Moon Pyramids (1st-7th C); Templo Mayor (Aztec, 42m base).[web:19][web:21][web:24]
- **Plazas**: Vast open courts for rituals/markets, e.g., Teotihuacan Citadel (400x200m), Tenochtitlan's Templo Mayor enclosure.[web:16][web:21]
- **Aztec Urbanism**: Tenochtitlan (1325-1521, 13km², 200k pop.): Lake island grid via causeways/chinampas; 4 quadrants, aqueducts, tezontle stone/adobe.[web:16][web:17][web:22]

**Fact-Check**: Teotihuacan pre-Aztec (1st-7th C); Aztecs postclassic.[web:18][web:21]
