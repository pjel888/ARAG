## Core Concepts (C-A-A Mnemonic – Repeat 3x Aloud)

**C-A-A**: **C**limate demands, **A**vailable materials, **A**dapted forms. Ties to PH: Verandas for ventilation, thick walls for thermal mass.

- **Overview**: European styles (Baroque, Georgian) morphed via local conditions into functional hybrids (16th-20th C).
- **Spanish Americas**: Churrigueresque Baroque (16th-18th C); adobe/stucco thick walls, internal patios for shade/cross-ventilation (e.g., Mexico, Peru).
- **English Americas (USA)**: Georgian symmetry (1700s) → Federal/Neoclassical (post-1776); Northern brick insulation; Southern raised floors + verandas for humidity/floods.
- **British Australia**: Georgian/Victorian (from 1788) → Federation (1901-1912); wide verandas, bull-nosed roofs, filigree screens for intense sun/rain.

**Fact-Check**: Americas from 1492+ Spanish/English; Australia British penal colony 1788; all verified evolutions, not "pure" imports.
