## Core Concepts (E-M-G Mnemonic – Chant 3x Now)

**E-M-G**: **E**arth basics, **M**ali Mosques, **G**reat Zimbabwe. Link to PH: Earth for thermal mass, Mali for ventilation, Zimbabwe for seismic flex.

- **Earth Architecture**: Prevalent in Africa's Sahel region (e.g., adobe bricks: sun-dried soil + organic stabilizers like straw; rammed earth: compacted moist soil in forms). **Core perk**: Thermal mass buffers extreme temps (\( \Delta 40^\circ \text{C} \) daily swings), keeping interiors cool.
- **Mali's Mosques**: Sudano-Sahelian style (e.g., Great Mosque of Djenné, orig. 13th C, rebuilt 1907; Djinguereber Mosque, 1327). Ferey mud-bricks; projecting toron wooden beams for annual replastering scaffolding; walls taper from 40cm base to reduce load/erosion.
- **Great Zimbabwe**: Shona-built stone complex (11th-15th C, ~18,000 peak pop., 720 ha). Dry-stone masonry (no mortar) with granite blocks, curved interlocking walls up to 11m (Hill Complex, Great Enclosure).

**Fact-Check**: Great Zimbabwe confirmed pre-colonial Shona via C14 dating (11th-15th C); Djenné style 13th C (UNESCO); no European origins.
