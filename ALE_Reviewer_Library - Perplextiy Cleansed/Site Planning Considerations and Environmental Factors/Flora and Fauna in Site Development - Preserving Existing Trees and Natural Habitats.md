# Flora and Fauna in Site Development: Preserving Existing Trees and Natural Habitats
*An Immersive ALE Review Module*

## The Real-World Scenario (The Hook)
Imagine you're a site developer tasked with constructing a new commercial complex. Your project is located near a protected wildlife sanctuary, which includes several endangered tree species. To proceed legally and ethically, you must conduct an Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) to evaluate the potential impacts on flora and fauna in your development area.

## Core Concepts
- **Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)**: A process that evaluates the environmental effects of a proposed project.
- **Protected Species**: Flora and fauna listed under national laws or regulations as needing special protection due to their endangered status.
- **Natural Habitats**: Areas designated by law for conservation, such as wildlife preserves, sanctuaries, and watershed reserves.

## ALE Exam Traps and Common Mistakes
- **Misunderstanding EIA Requirements**: Failing to conduct a thorough environmental impact assessment can lead to legal penalties or project delays.
- **Ignoring Local Regulations**: Not adhering to local government regulations regarding land use planning and protected areas may result in non-compliance issues.
