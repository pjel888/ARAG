## Additional Details

### Hydrology in Site Planning: Surface Drainage, Swales, and Stormwater Runoff

Hydrological considerations are crucial during site planning to ensure proper management of surface water. According to Rule IX - SANITATION (PD 1096 RIRR 2004), the storm drainage system is a critical component in managing surface runoff effectively.

#### Surface Drainage
Surface drainage involves directing and controlling rainwater or snowmelt over land surfaces, ensuring that it does not cause flooding or erosion. This includes designing systems to manage water flow across various terrains within a site (PD 1096 RIRR 2004 - Revised IRR).

#### Swales
Swales are shallow channels designed for the conveyance and treatment of stormwater runoff, often used in low-impact development practices. Although not explicitly mentioned in Philippine laws or regulations provided here, swales play a significant role in managing surface water by reducing peak flows and improving water quality.

#### Stormwater Runoff
Stormwater management is essential to prevent flooding and erosion while protecting natural resources from pollution caused by urban runoff. Strategies include the installation of collection facilities such as detention basins, retention ponds, or infiltration systems (Russ - Site Planning and Design Handbook).

These elements are integral in ensuring that site planning adheres to environmental standards and regulations set forth for sustainable development practices.
