## Additional Details

Juan Nakpil was a prominent architect during the Golden Age of Philippine architecture, known for his contributions to the design and construction of numerous significant buildings. His specific architectural works include the San Sebastian Church in Manila, which is notable for its unique iron structure that survived multiple earthquakes.

Pablo Antonio also played an important role as an engineer and planner during this period. He was involved in various infrastructure projects across the country but his most recognized contribution lies in the planning of urban spaces such as parks and recreational areas, enhancing public amenities through thoughtful design principles.

Leandro Locsin is renowned for integrating traditional Filipino elements with modern architectural concepts. His work on the Cultural Center of the Philippines (CCP) Complex stands out due to its innovative use of space and materials that reflect both local culture and international trends in architecture at the time.

Francisco Manosa made significant contributions through his expertise in structural engineering, particularly in designing buildings capable of resisting natural disasters like earthquakes. His work on reinforced concrete structures has set standards for safety and durability in Philippine construction practices.
