# Minimum Parking Requirements per Occupancy Classification
*An Immersive ALE Review Module*

## The Real-World Scenario (The Hook)
Imagine you are designing a new residential subdivision with single-family and multi-family dwelling units in Quezon City. According to PD 1096 RIRR, what minimum parking requirements must be met for each type of unit based on their lot sizes?

## Core Concepts
- **Occupancy Classification**: Group A Units (single-detached or attached dwellings), Single-Family/Multi-Family Dwelling Units.
- **Parking Slot Requirement**: Minimum number of off-street cum on-site parking slots required per dwelling units and lots.

## The Hard Data (Cheat Sheet)
| Occupancy Type | Lot Size Range (sq. m) / Gross Floor Area (GFA, sq. m) | Parking Slots Required |
|----------------|---------------------------------------------------------|------------------------|
| Group A Units  | 32.00 to 72.00 / 18.00 to 22.00                         | One pooled slot per six lots or units with GFA between 18.00 and 22.00 sq. m |
| Single-Family/Multi-Family Dwelling Units | 50.00 to 96.00 / 30.00 to 42.00 | One slot per every three lots or units with GFA between 30.00 and 42.00 sq. m |
| Nightclubs, Super Clubs & Theater-Restaurants | 18.00-40.00 / >70.00 | One pooled slot for each unit (or fraction thereof) up to 40.00 sq. m; one per unit beyond that |

## ALE Exam Traps and Common Mistakes
1. **Misinterpreting Lot Size vs GFA**: Ensure you correctly identify whether the requirement is based on lot size or gross floor area.
2. **Ignoring Prohibitions**: Remember that conversion/change of use, reduction in parking spaces, encroachment on RROW are prohibited.

## Active Recall Question
What minimum number of pooled off-street cum on-site parking slots must be provided for every six lots or units with a gross floor area between 18.00 to 22.00 sq. meters according to PD 1096 RIRR (2004)?

A) One slot per three lots
B) One pooled slot per six lots 
C) Two slots per four lots

> **Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** According to the rules provided in PD 1096 RIRR, Group A Units require one pooled off-street cum on-site parking slot for every six lots or units with a gross floor area between 18.00 and 22.00 sq. meters.

## Additional Details

- **Minimum Parking Requirements per Occupancy Classification**: The PD 1096 RIRR 2004 - Revised IRR (DPWH Official) provides specific parking requirements based on occupancy classifications. For instance, Group A units with a gross floor area between 18.00 to 22.00 sq. meters require one pooled off-street cum on-site parking slot for every six lots or dwelling units [PD 1096 RIRR 2004 - Revised IRR (DPWH Official).pdf | page 97]. Single-family and multi-family dwelling units with a gross floor area between 50.00 to 96.00 sq. meters require one parking slot per every three lots or units [PD 1096 RIRR 2004 - Revised IRR (DPWH Official).pdf | page 97]. Nightclubs and theater-restaurants with a gross floor area of 18.00–40.00 sq. m require one parking slot per every two units, while larger areas need one slot per unit or fraction thereof beyond 70.00 sq. m [PD 1096 IRR - Full Implementing Rules and Regulations.pdf | page 106]. Additionally, columbariums are required to provide one car slot for each compartment niche [PD 1096 RIRR 2004 - Revised IRR (Architecture Board).pdf | page 107].

- **Integral Parking Slot Requirements**: The parking slots must be an integral part of the building or structure, and any provided outside will only count as buffer parking. Furthermore, there are prohibitions against conversion/change of use/occupancy, reduction in parking spaces, encroachment on RROW (Right-of-Way), handling public utility vehicles [PD 1096 RIRR 2004 - Revised IRR (Architecture Board).pdf | page 111].
