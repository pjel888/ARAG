# Building Permit Process, Exemptions, Fines, and the Role of the Building Official

_An Immersive Architect Licensure Exam (ALE) Review Module_

## The Real-World Scenario (The Hook)

Imagine you are an architect tasked with designing a new public library in Manila. You need to understand how building permits work, what exemptions apply, potential fines for violations, and your interactions with the local government's Building Official.

## Core Concepts

- **Building Permit Process**: Applications are reviewed by the Building Official for compliance with zoning, structural, fire safety, and sanitary standards before permit issuance.
- **Exemptions**: Specific structures like public buildings (fees only) and traditional indigenous family dwellings (permit entirely) per PD 1096 IRR §302.
- **Fines**: Graded penalties under Rule XVIII for violations from missing signs (₱5,000) to grave offenses (₱20,000+ or jail).
- **Role of the Building Official**: Reviews applications, inspects sites, issues permits/orders, and enforces via fines/imprisonment (PD 1096 §212).

## The Hard Data (Cheat Sheet)

| Concept                     | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Building Permit Process** | - Submit complete plans/docs to Office of Building Official (OBO)<br>- Review for compliance (zoning, NBC standards) within 15 working days<br>- Pay fees → Issue permit if compliant (PD 1096 IRR §305) |
| **Exemptions**              | - Public buildings: Exempt from **fees** but **require permit** (IRR §302(a)(1))<br>- Traditional indigenous family dwellings: Exempt from **securing permit** (IRR §302(a)(2))                          |
| **Fines**                   | - **Light**: No signs/safety measures → ₱5,000 (Rule XVIII §1801)<br>- **Less Grave**: No stoppage compliance → ₱10,000-20,000<br>- **Grave**: Unauthorized major work → ₱20,000+ or 6mo-2yrs jail       |

## ALE Exam Traps and Common Mistakes

1. **Exemption Scope**: Forgetting public buildings need permits (just no fees); assuming all "public" = fully exempt.
2. **Fine Levels**: Mixing light (₱5K, signs) vs. grave (₱20K+, structure); underestimating jail for serious cases.
3. **Timeline**: No automatic "15 days post-payment"—requires full compliance first.

## Active Recall Question

Which is exempt from **building permit fees** (but not the permit itself) under PD 1096?

A) Residential buildings  
B) Commercial establishments  
C) Public buildings  
D) Industrial facilities

<details>
  <summary>Answer</summary>
  C) Public buildings. Per IRR §302(a)(1), public buildings are exempt from fees but must secure permits. Traditional indigenous dwellings are fully exempt from permits (§302(a)(2)).
</details>

## Additional Details

- **Full Exemption List** (IRR §302): Public bldgs (fees only); indigenous dwellings; monuments; private piers <3m; etc.
- **Process Flow**: Application → Review (15 wd) → Payment → Permit (or denial w/reason) → Post-permit inspections.
