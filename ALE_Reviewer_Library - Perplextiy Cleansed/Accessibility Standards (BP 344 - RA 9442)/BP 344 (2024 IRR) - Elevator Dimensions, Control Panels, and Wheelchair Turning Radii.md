# 🏛️ BP 344 (2024 IRR): Elevator Dimensions, Controls & Turning Spaces

_An Immersive ALE Review Module – Built for Maximum Retention_

## 🚀 The Real-World Hook (Anchor It Visually)

You're the architect for a 10-story mall. A wheelchair user calls the elevator. If the door is too narrow or the car is too tight, they are stranded. **BP 344 failure = an inaccessible building + an ALE score of zero.** Visualize a 1.2 x 1.4m "wheelchair taxi" pulling into a massive 1.8m circular lobby. To lock this into your brain, use the **E-D-C-T Framework**.

## 🎯 The E-D-C-T Framework (Chunking & Mnemonics)

_Read this aloud twice to build auditory memory:_

- **E (Elevator)**: **1200** W x **1400** D mm (Clear floor space).
- **D (Door)**: **900** mm (Clear opening).
- **C (Controls)**: **900–1200** mm height, **20** mm buttons with Braille.
- **T (Turning)**: **1800 x 1800** mm (Lobby maneuver circle).

## ⚠️ The Board Exam Traps (Error Management Training)

Examiners love to test if you know the _exact_ wording and the _newest_ rules. **Self-Test These Pitfalls Now:**

1.  **The "Old Rule" Trap:** The options are (A) 1100x1400, (B) 1200x1400.
    - ❌ _Don't pick A!_ 1100 is the dead, pre-2024 rule. **1200x1400 mm** is the new law.
2.  **The "Overall" vs "Clear" Trap:** "Elevator door is 900 mm wide overall."
    - ❌ The law states 900 mm **CLEAR opening**. The physical door/frame will be wider.
3.  **The "Car Turning" Trap:** "Turning space inside the elevator."
    - ❌ The **1800x1800 mm** turning space is in the **Lobby/Corridor**, not inside the 1200x1400 car!
4.  **The "Max Height" Trap:** "Highest button is 1300 mm."
    - ❌ Maximum reach height is strictly **1200 mm AFF**.
