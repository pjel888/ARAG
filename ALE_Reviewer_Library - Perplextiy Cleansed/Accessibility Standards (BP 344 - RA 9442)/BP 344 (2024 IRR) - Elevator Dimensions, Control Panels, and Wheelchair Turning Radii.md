## 🎯 The E-D-C-T-J Framework (Mnemonic Chunking)

_Read this aloud twice and visualize the physical space to build spatial memory:_

- **E (Elevator Car)**: **1200** mm Width x **1400** mm Depth (Clear floor space).
- **D (Door & Distance)**: **900** mm clear opening. Max **30** meters from the main entrance.
- **C (Controls)**: **900–1200** mm height, **20** mm buttons.
- **T (Turning Space)**: **1800 x 1800** mm (Lobby maneuver circle).
- **J (Jambs)**: Braille indicators at exactly **1200** mm on both side door jambs.

## ⚠️ The Board Exam Traps (Error Management Training)

Examiners love to test if you know the _exact_ wording and the _newest_ rules. **Self-Test These Pitfalls Now:**

1.  **The "Old Rule" Trap:** The options are (A) 1100x1400, (B) 1200x1400.
    - ❌ _Don't pick A!_ 1100 is the dead, pre-2024 rule. **1200x1400 mm** is the law.
2.  **The "Overall" vs "Clear" Trap:** "Elevator door is 900 mm wide overall."
    - ❌ The law states 900 mm **CLEAR opening**. The physical door/frame is wider.
3.  **The "Car Turning" Trap:** "Turning space inside the elevator is 1.80m."
    - ❌ The **1800x1800 mm** turning space is in the **Lobby/Corridor**, not inside the 1200x1400 car!
4.  **The "Jamb vs Panel" Trap:** "Braille is only required on the buttons."
    - ❌ Floor level indicators in Braille MUST be installed on **both side jambs** of the elevator door at 1200 mm height.
