## 🧮 Contrast Math Drill (Exam Calculator Ready)

**Q: Sign luminance check**

- Background: 50 cd/m²
- Sign: 75 cd/m²
- Formula: **|(75-50)/(75+50)| × 100 = 16.7%** ❌ **FAIL** (<30%)

**Pro Tip**: Always test **sign vs background**, not sign vs wall!
