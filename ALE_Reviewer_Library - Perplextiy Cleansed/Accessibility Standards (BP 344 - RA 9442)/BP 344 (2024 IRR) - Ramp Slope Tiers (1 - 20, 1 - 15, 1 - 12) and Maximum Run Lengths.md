## 🚀 The Real-World Hook (Sensory & Spatial Anchoring)

You're designing a hospital ramp. A wheelchair stalls halfway up because the slope is too steep, or the user rolls backwards because you forgot the landing. **Failure = an inaccessible building + a board exam score of zero.** For the ALE, we anchor on the **2024 IRR standards**: "20-GOLD, 15-MAX".

## 🎯 Core Framework (The S-L-L Method)

Master any ramp by breaking it down into **S**lope, **L**ength, and **L**anding.
_Read this aloud twice to build auditory memory:_

- **S (Slope)**: **1:20** (Preferred/Gold) or **1:15** (Maximum Limit).
- **L (Length/Run)**: How far it goes before a rest is legally required (Max **10.50m** for 1:15).
- **L (Landing)**: The flat resting zone (**1800 mm** minimum length).

**Universal Formula**: `Length = Rise × Slope Ratio`

## ⚠️ The Board Exam Traps (Error Management Training)

Examiners test if you know the _exact_ numbers and the _current_ memos. **Self-Test These Pitfalls:**

1.  **The "9-Meter" Trap:** "Maximum run for 1:15 is 9m."
    - ❌ _False!_ 9 meters (30 feet) is the American ADA standard. The Philippine 2024 IRR states the max length is **10.50 meters**.
2.  **The "1500mm Landing" Trap:** "Landings must be 1.5m."
    - ❌ _False!_ The 2024 IRR explicitly upgraded intermediate, top, and bottom landings to a minimum length of **1800 mm**.
3.  **The "1:12 is Illegal" Trap (The Memo 03 Curveball):** "1:12 is strictly prohibited."
    - ❌ _Context Matters!_ While the 2024 IRR pushes for 1:15/1:20, **DPWH NBCDO Memo Circular No. 03 s. 2025** states that **1:12 is still the enforceable standard** for permits until the new appendix is officially published in the Official Gazette. Default to 1:15/1:20 for theoretical design, but know that 1:12 is technically still alive for strict permit questions!

## 🧮 Computation Drill (Cognitive Load Reduction)

_Do the math. The universal formula (`Length = Rise x Ratio`) works anywhere._
**Exam Q**: You have a 1400 mm total elevation to overcome using a 1:15 slope. What is the total spatial footprint of the ramp system?

- **Step 1 (Find total sloped length):** 1400 mm (1.4 m) × 15 = **21 meters**.
- **Step 2 (Check max rise per run):** The 2024 limit is **700 mm**.
- **Step 3 (Calculate runs needed):** 1400 ÷ 700 = **2 runs** (each run is 10.5m long).
- **Step 4 (Find landings):** 2 runs require **1 intermediate landing** (min 1800 mm). You ALSO need a **top** and **bottom** landing (1800 mm each).
- **Step 5 (Calculate total footprint):** \* Top Landing: 1.8 m
  - Sloped Run 1: 10.5 m
  - Intermediate Landing: 1.8 m
  - Sloped Run 2: 10.5 m
  - Bottom Landing: 1.8 m
  - **TOTAL FOOTPRINT = 26.4 meters** _(Don't fall for the trap of forgetting the top/bottom clearances!)_
