# BP 344 (2024 IRR): Ramp Slope Tiers (1:20, 1:15)

_An Immersive ALE Review Module_

## The Real-World Scenario (The Hook)

Imagine you are designing an accessible ramp for a public building. You need to ensure that the slope and length of your ramp comply with BP 344's IRR requirements to accommodate individuals using wheelchairs or mobility aids.

## Core Concepts

- **Gradient**: The steepness of the ramp, measured as rise over run (1:20 = 5%, 1:15 = 6.67%).
- **Maximum Slope**: 1:20 (preferred); 1:15 (max allowed) per 2024 IRR.
- **Maximum Length and Rise**: Limits before intermediate landings required.

## The Hard Data (Cheat Sheet)

| Maximum Slope | Maximum Length | Maximum Rise | Landing Interval |
| ------------- | -------------- | ------------ | ---------------- |
| 1:20 (5%)     | Unlimited\*    | Per code     | Every 15 m       |
| 1:15 (6.67%)  | 9 m            | 600 mm       | Every 9 m        |

_\*No horizontal length limit if rise ≤600 mm continuously; landings required at intervals [IRR App. A, Sec. 5.2, p. 17]_

## Step-by-Step Computation (If Applicable)

**Example: 1:15 ramp for 1200 mm rise**

1. Rise per segment: max 600 mm
2. Length per segment: 600 mm × 15 = 9 m
3. Segments needed: 1200 ÷ 600 = 2
4. Total length: 2 × 9 m + landing = 18+ m

## ALE Exam Traps and Common Mistakes

- **Wrong max length**: 1:15 max = 9 m (not 10.5 m); rise ≤600 mm per run.
- **No 1:12 slope**: 2024 IRR prohibits 1:12 (8.33%); only 1:20/1:15 allowed.
- **Missing landings**: Required every 9 m (1:15) or 15 m (1:20); min 1500x1500 mm.

## Active Recall Question

What is the maximum length for an accessible ramp with a gradient of 1:15 according to BP 344 (2024 IRR)?

A. 6 m  
B. 700 mm  
C. 10.50 m  
D. 9 m

> **Answer:** D. 9 m
>
> **Explanation:** 2024 IRR Appendix A, Section 5.2 (p. 17) limits 1:15 ramps to 9 m horizontal (600 mm rise) before intermediate landing.

## Additional Details

- **Landings**: Min 1500x1500 mm clear; level with ramp surface [Sec. 5.3, p. 18].
- **No 1:12 Allowed**: Pedro Santos slides are illustrative only; official IRR caps at 1:15 max. Steeper requires stairs + lift.
- **Handrails**: Continuous, 800-900 mm AFF both sides; extended 300 mm beyond top/bottom [Sec. 5.4, p. 18].
