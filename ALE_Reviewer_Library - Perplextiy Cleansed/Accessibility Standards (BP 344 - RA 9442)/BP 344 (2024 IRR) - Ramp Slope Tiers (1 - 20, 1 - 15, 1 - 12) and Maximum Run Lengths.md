# BP 344 (2024 IRR): Ramp Slope Tiers (1:20, 1:15)
*An Immersive ALE Review Module*

## The Real-World Scenario (The Hook)
Imagine you are designing an accessible ramp for a public building. You need to ensure that the slope and length of your ramp comply with BP 344's IRR requirements to accommodate individuals using wheelchairs or mobility aids.

## Core Concepts
- **Gradient**: The steepness of the ramp, measured as rise over run.
- **Maximum Slope**: The steepest allowable gradient for ramps under BP 344 (2024 IRR).
- **Maximum Length and Rise**: The longest permissible distance a single ramp can span before requiring an intermediate landing.

## ALE Exam Traps and Common Mistakes
- **Overlooking the need for intermediate landings**: Ramps longer than 10.50 meters must have an interval landing of at least 1800 mm.
- **Incorrectly applying slope tiers**: Ensure that steeper slopes (e.g., 1:12) are not used unless explicitly allowed by a newer official source.
