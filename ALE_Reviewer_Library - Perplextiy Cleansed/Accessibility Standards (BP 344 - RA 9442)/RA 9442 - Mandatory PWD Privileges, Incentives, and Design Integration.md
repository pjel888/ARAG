# ⚖️ PWD Law Family: Social Benefits (RA 9442/10754) vs. Physical Blueprints (BP 344)

_An Immersive ALE Review Module – Mastering Legislative Jurisdiction & Financial Incentives_

## 🚀 The Real-World Hook (The Architect's Scope Trap)

Imagine you are in the Professional Practice exam. The question asks: _"Under which law is a building owner entitled to a tax deduction for installing an accessible elevator?"_ If you answer "BP 344," you fail the question.
**Why?** BP 344 tells you _how_ to build it, but **RA 7277** (The Magna Carta) is what gives the owner the _money_ back. Mixing up "Regulatory Specs" (Blueprints) with "Social/Financial Rights" (Benefits) is the #1 reason students lose easy points in Area 3.

## 🎯 The "3 S" Framework (Cognitive Compartmentalization)

_To prevent "Schema Overlap," separate the laws by their functional impact on the Architect:_

1.  **S (Social & Savings) – RA 9442 & RA 10754**: These focus on the PWD's wallet and dignity (20% Discount, VAT Exemption, Express Lanes, Anti-Ridicule).
2.  **S (Structural & Salaries) – RA 7277**: The "Parent Law." It gives the _Architect_ a selling point: **Tax Deductions** for the client (50% for building mods, 25% for hiring PWDs).
3.  **S (Specs & Spaces) – BP 344**: The "Bible of Dimensions." This is the only law that cares about millimeters, slopes, and turning circles.

## 📊 The "Benefits vs. Blueprints" Matrix (Contrastive Learning)

_FACT-CHECK: RA 10754 is the "VAT Law." RA 9442 is the "Anti-Ridicule Law." BP 344 is the "Dimension Law."_

## ⚠️ The Professional Practice Traps (Error Management Training)

Examiners love to test the **Tax Incentive** numbers because students always swap them. **Memorize these now:**

1.  **The "50% vs 25%" Trap:**
    - Client modifies a building for PWDs? They get **50% of the cost** as a tax deduction (RA 7277).
    - Client hires a PWD? They get **25% of the wages** as a tax deduction (RA 7277).
2.  **The "Anti-Ridicule" Trivia:** If a question asks about the "Public Ridicule/Vilification" of PWDs, the answer is **RA 9442**. It was the first law to criminalize making fun of a person's disability.
3.  **The "VAT" update:** If the question asks which law added the **VAT Exemption** on top of the 20% discount, the answer is **RA 10754**.
4.  **The "Jurisdiction" Fallacy:** \* _Question:_ "What is the slope of a ramp under RA 9442?"
    - _Response:_ **None.** RA 9442 does not define slopes. BP 344 does. Don't fall for the "Laws-as-Keywords" trap.

## 🧮 Computation Drill (The "Architect-as-Consultant" Logic)

**Scenario:** Your client spends **PHP 1,000,000** to install a BP 344-compliant elevator in an old building.

- **Architect's Advice:** "Sir/Ma'am, under **RA 7277**, you can deduct **PHP 500,000** (50% of the cost) from your net taxable income as a structural improvement incentive."
- **The Trap:** If you cited BP 344 as the source of the tax break, you would be providing incorrect legal advice.

## 🧠 Active Recall Battery (The Testing Effect)

**Q1:** Which law prohibits the public ridicule and vilification of PWDs?

> **Ans:** RA 9442.

**Q2:** A restaurant owner modifies their entrance to be accessible. What percentage of the direct cost of the modification can they deduct from their taxable income?

> **Ans:** 50% (Under RA 7277).

**Q3:** Does RA 10754 provide a physical dimension for the width of a priority/express lane?

> **Ans:** No. It provides the VAT exemption. Express lanes are mandated by RA 9442, but their physical specs follow BP 344 (1.20m min width).

**Q4:** What is the employer's tax incentive for paying a salary of 20,000 to a PWD employee?

> **Ans:** An additional 25% deduction (5,000) from their gross income (Under RA 7277).
