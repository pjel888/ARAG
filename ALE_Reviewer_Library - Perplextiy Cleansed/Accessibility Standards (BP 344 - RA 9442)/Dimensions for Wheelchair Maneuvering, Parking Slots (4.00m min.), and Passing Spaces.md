# ♿ Universal Wheelchair Dimensions: Parking, Passing & Turning Spaces

_An Immersive Review Module – Built for Spatial Mastery & Schema Updating_

## 🚀 The Real-World Hook (The Transfer Gap)

Imagine a wheelchair user in a van. They park, but the aisle is only 1.20 m wide. They open their door, and the ramp hits a bollard or another car. They are trapped. **BP 344 failure = an unusable building + a board exam score of zero.** To design correctly, you must visualize the **"Transfer Zone."** We anchor on the **2024 Revised Standards**: "4-12-18".

## 🎯 The 4-12-18 Master Rule (Chunking & Mnemonics)

_Read this aloud. These are your three "Holy Grail" numbers for site planning:_

- **4.0 (Parking)**: **4000 mm** total width (Includes the 1500 mm access aisle).
- **1.2 (Pathway)**: **1200 mm** clear width (The absolute baseline for all walkways).
- **1.8 (Turning)**: **1800 mm** diameter (The new universal turning circle).

## ⚠️ The Board Exam Traps (Error Management Training)

Examiners will bait you with numbers that _used_ to be correct. **Dodge these specific traps:**

1.  **The "3.70 m" Trap:** One option for parking width is 3.70 m.
    - ❌ **Discard it.** That was the 1994 standard. The 2024 Revised IRR mandates **4.00 m**.
2.  **The "Plus 1.5" Trap:** "A 4000 mm parking slot needs an additional 1500 mm aisle."
    - ❌ **False.** The 4.0 m _already includes_ the aisle. Don't double-count and waste site space!
3.  **The "1.50 m Turn" Trap:** "The turning space required in a restroom is 1.50 m."
    - ❌ **False.** The 2024 upgrade standardized turning spaces to **1.80 m x 1.80 m**.
4.  **The "Centerline" Trap:** "The International Symbol of Access (ISA) is at the top of the slot."
    - ❌ **False.** The pavement ISA must be **centered** in the parking slot (size: 1.0 m to 1.5 m).

## 🧮 Computation Drill (Spatial Math)

**Exam Q**: You are designing a parking lot with two _adjacent_ accessible slots. What is the total width required?

- **Step 1:** Slot A (2.5m) + Shared Aisle (1.5m) + Slot B (2.5m).
- **Step 2:** 2.5 + 1.5 + 2.5 = **6.5 meters total**.
- **The Key:** Two PWD slots can **share** one 1500 mm access aisle to save space.
