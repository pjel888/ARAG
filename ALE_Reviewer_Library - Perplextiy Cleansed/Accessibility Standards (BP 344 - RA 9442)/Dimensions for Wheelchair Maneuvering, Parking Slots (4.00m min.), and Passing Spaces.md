## The Real-World Scenario (The Hook)
Imagine designing a new commercial building that must accommodate wheelchair users effectively. You need to ensure there are sufficient accessible parking slots with proper dimensions, clear walkways between parked cars, and adequate passing spaces in corridors.

## Core Concepts
- **Accessible Parking Slots**: Minimum width of 4000 mm (4 meters) and length of 5000 mm (5 meters).
- **Walkway Between Parked Cars**: Clear width of at least 1200 mm.
- **Passing Spaces in Corridors**: Minimum clear width of 1500 mm.

## Additional Details

### Dimensions for Wheelchair Maneuvering
The provision of adequate space for wheelchair maneuvering generally ensures that disabled persons equipped with other technical aids or accompanied by assistants have sufficient room. According to PD 1096 RIRR, the length of wheelchairs varies from 1.10 m to 1.80 m and the width ranges between 0.60 m and 0.75 m. A circle with a diameter of 1.50 meters is recommended for planning wheelchair turning spaces.

### Parking Slots
Accessible parking slots should have a minimum width of 4000 mm (4 meters) and length of 5000 mm (5 meters). The additional space ensures that adjacent pathways adequately accommodate wheelchair passage, as per BP 344 IRR. 

### Passing Spaces
Passing spaces are essential for maneuverability in areas where wheelchairs or other mobility aids need to pass safely alongside parked vehicles or obstacles. These should be designed considering the dimensions of wheelchairs and additional space required for transferring from a vehicle.
