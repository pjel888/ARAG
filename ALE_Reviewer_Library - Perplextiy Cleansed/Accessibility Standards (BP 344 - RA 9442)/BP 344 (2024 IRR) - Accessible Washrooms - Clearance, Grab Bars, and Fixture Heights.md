# BP 344 (2024 IRR): Accessible Washrooms - Clearance, Grab Bars, and Fixture Heights
*An Immersive ALE Review Module*

## The Real-World Scenario (The Hook)
Imagine you are designing an accessible washroom for a new public building. You need to ensure that the toilet cubicles meet BP 344 requirements in terms of clearance space, grab bar installation, and fixture heights.

## Core Concepts
- **Accessible Toilet Cubicle**: A designated area with necessary dimensions and fixtures designed specifically for persons with disabilities.
- **Grab Bars**: Fixed L-type and flip-up bars installed to assist individuals during transfers or while using the toilet.
- **Fixture Heights**: Accessories such as mirrors and lavatories must be mounted at specific heights to ensure ease of use.

## The Hard Data (Cheat Sheet)
| Item | Dimension/Requirement |
|------|-----------------------|
| Accessible Toilet Cubicle Area | 1800 mm x 1800 mm [amended_irr_bp_344_official_gazette_2.pdf, page 50] |
| Turning Space Outside Water Closet | 1800 mm x 1800 mm [amended_irr_bp_344_official_gazette_2.pdf, page 50] |
| Toilet Seat Height | 450 mm from finished floor to centerline of water closet seat [OFFICIAL_FACT_CARD] |
| Grab Bars (L-Type) | Installed on side wall with horizontal leg extending 300 mm and vertical leg starting at back wall. Top edge is 300 mm above toilet seat [amended_irr_bp_344_official_gazette_2.pdf, page 51] |
| Grab Bars (Flip-Up) | Installed on opposite side of L-type grab bar with top edge also 300 mm above the toilet seat. Clear distance from face to centerline is 350 mm [OFFICIAL_FACT_CARD] |
| Accessories and Fixtures Height | Mounted at a height of 1000 mm above finished floor for easy reach [amended_irr_bp_344_official_gazette_2.pdf, page 51] |
| Mirrors | At least one mirror with bottom edge not more than 1000 mm from the finished floor or tilted for wheelchair use [OFFICIAL_FACT_CARD] |

## ALE Exam Traps and Common Mistakes
- **Misinterpreting Fixture Heights**: Ensure that all fixtures are mounted at 1000 mm above the finished floor, not just mirrors.
- **Omitting Turning Space Dimensions**: The turning space outside water closet cubicles must be a minimum of 1800 mm x 1800 mm to allow for wheelchair maneuvering.
- **Incorrect Grab Bar Placement and Specifications**: Both L-type and flip-up grab bars should have their top edges at the correct height (300 mm above toilet seat) with proper clearances.

## Additional Details

- **Emergency call button placement**: Emergency call buttons shall be mounted between 400 mm and 600 mm above the finished floor to ensure they are easily reachable for individuals in need. (Source: amended_irr_bp_344_official_gazette_2.pdf, page not specified)
  
- **Shower compartments**: Roll-in shower dimensions shall be a minimum of 1800 mm x 1800 mm and must include L-shaped bars to provide support for wheelchair users. Additionally, grab bar requirements specify that two slip-resistant grab bars are necessary: an L-type with the horizontal leg extending from the back wall by at least 300 mm towards the front of the water closet; a vertical grab bar installed on the shower side of the bathtub or roll-in compartment. (Source: amended_irr_bp_344_official_gazette_2.pdf, page 53)
