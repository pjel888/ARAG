## Additional Details

### African Vernacular: Earth Architecture
Earth architecture is a traditional building technique that utilizes soil as the primary construction material. This method has been widely used across various regions, including Africa, where it offers an environmentally sustainable approach to housing and community development. In many parts of sub-Saharan Africa, earth buildings are constructed using adobe bricks or rammed earth techniques, which provide natural insulation against extreme temperatures.

### Mosques of Mali
The mosques in Mali exemplify the rich architectural heritage of West African Islamic culture. Notable among them is the Djinguereber Mosque located in Timbuktu, a UNESCO World Heritage site known for its intricate mud-brick construction and significant historical importance as one of the earliest universities in Africa during the 14th century.

### Great Zimbabwe
Great Zimbabwe stands as an iconic example of pre-colonial African urban culture. This ancient city-state was built between the 11th and 15th centuries AD, featuring massive stone structures that showcase advanced engineering skills without the use of mortar or metal tools. The site is recognized for its cultural significance in understanding the history of southern Africa's Bantu-speaking peoples.
