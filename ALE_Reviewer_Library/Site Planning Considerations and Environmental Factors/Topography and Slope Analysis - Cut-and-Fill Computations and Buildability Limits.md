## Core Concepts
- **Topography**: The study of landforms and features on the Earth's surface.
- **Slope Analysis**: Evaluating the steepness or gradient of a terrain to ensure compliance with building codes and standards.
- **Cut-and-Fill Computations**: Calculating earthwork volumes for construction projects, including removal (cut) and addition (fill) of soil.
