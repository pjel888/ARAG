## Additional Details

The following details provide additional context on Amihan/Habagat wind paths, solar orientation strategies, and specific microclimate analysis techniques that were previously missing from the article.

- **Amihan (Northeastern Winds) and Habagat (Southwestern Winds)**: These seasonal winds significantly influence site design in the Philippines. During the northeast monsoon season, Amihan brings cool air from the north to southeast direction, while during the southwest monsoon season, Habagat carries moisture-laden air from the south to northwest direction. Understanding these wind patterns is crucial for designing buildings that maximize natural ventilation and minimize heat gain.

- **Solar Orientation Strategies**: Effective solar orientation strategies include positioning buildings to take advantage of prevailing winds and minimizing exposure to direct sunlight during peak hours. Techniques such as incorporating overhangs, louvers, or shading devices can help reduce the impact of afternoon sun while allowing for adequate daylight penetration.

These additional points enhance the coverage on microclimate analysis by providing specific techniques and considerations that are essential in Philippine construction practices.
