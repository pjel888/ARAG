*An Immersive ALE Review Module*

## The Real-World Scenario (The Hook)
Imagine you are tasked with designing a new residential building in an area prone to geological hazards. You need to ensure that your design complies with all necessary regulations regarding fault lines, liquefaction zones, and soil bearing capacities.

## Core Concepts
- **Geological Hazards**: Potential risks such as earthquakes, landslides, and subsidence.
- **Identifying Fault Lines**: Determining the location of active or potential earthquake faults.
- **Liquefaction Zones**: Areas where ground shaking can cause liquefaction during an earthquake.
- **Soil Bearing Capacities**: The maximum load that soil can support without failing.

## Additional Details

### Liquefaction Zones
The identification of liquefaction zones is critical for ensuring structural safety in areas prone to geological hazards, particularly those susceptible to soil liquefaction. According to PD 1096 RIRR (2004), adequate soil exploration including boring and load tests are required at sites with potential geotechnical risks. However, specific methodologies or figures provided by the National Structural Code of the Philippines (NSCP) should be consulted for detailed guidelines on determining liquefaction zones.

### Soil Bearing Capacities
For buildings/structures located in areas with geological/geotechnical hazards, soil bearing capacities must be accurately determined to ensure structural integrity and safety. The PD 1096 RIRR mandates that civil or geotechnical engineers provide a written report including design bearing capacity figures along with test results when applying for building permits. These reports should adhere to the latest approved provisions of NSCP which detail methodologies for assessing soil conditions, conducting necessary tests (such as boring and load tests), and determining appropriate bearing capacities based on site-specific characteristics.

Consulting the National Structural Code of the Philippines (NSCP) is essential for obtaining exact figures or methods related to soil bearing capacities. This document provides comprehensive guidelines tailored to Philippine geotechnical contexts, ensuring that structural designs account for local conditions accurately.
