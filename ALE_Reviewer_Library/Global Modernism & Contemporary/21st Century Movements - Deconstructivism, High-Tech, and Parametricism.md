## Core Concepts
**Deconstructivism:** An architectural movement characterized by fragmented forms, non-linear designs, and unconventional use of materials.
- **High-Tech Architecture:** A style emphasizing industrial aesthetics with exposed mechanical systems and innovative structural solutions.
- **Parametricism:** Utilizes computer algorithms to generate complex geometries that respond dynamically to design parameters.

## ALE Exam Traps and Common Mistakes
**Common Misconceptions:**
1. Confusing the characteristics of Deconstructivism with those of Brutalism.
2. Overlooking the industrial aesthetic elements in High-Tech Architecture.
3. Failing to understand how parametric design uses algorithms.

**Exam Tips:**
- Be familiar with key architects associated with each movement (e.g., Frank Gehry for Deconstructivism, Norman Foster for High-Tech).
- Recognize examples of buildings that embody these styles when presented in exam questions.
