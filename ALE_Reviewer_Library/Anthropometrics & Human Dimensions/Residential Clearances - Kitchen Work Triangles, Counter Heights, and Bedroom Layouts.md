# Residential Clearances: Kitchen Work Triangles, Counter Heights, and Bedroom Layouts
*An Immersive ALE Review Module*

## The Real-World Scenario (The Hook)
Imagine you're designing a new residential building in the Philippines. You need to ensure that all repairs are done according to local regulations without affecting structural integrity. Additionally, understanding when partial occupancy permits can be issued is crucial for project timelines.

## Core Concepts
- **Permits and Repairs**: Repair works not involving any structural members do not require additional permits.
- **Certificate of Occupancy (CO)**: A CO must be obtained before a building or structure can be used. Partial certificates may be issued for completed sections prior to full completion.

## Additional Details

### Kitchen Work Triangles
The concept of kitchen work triangles is a design principle that focuses on efficiency in food preparation. It involves positioning three main components—refrigerator, stove/oven, and sink—in such a way that they form an efficient triangle with no obstacles or major traffic areas within the path between them. This layout minimizes walking distance while maximizing convenience for cooking tasks.

### Counter Heights
Counter heights are crucial in kitchen design as they affect comfort and usability during food preparation activities. Standard counter height is typically 36 inches (91 cm) above the floor, but this can vary based on individual needs or specific requirements of certain appliances like cooktops which may require a lower surface for safety reasons.

### Bedroom Layouts
Bedroom layouts should prioritize functionality and comfort while adhering to space constraints. Key elements include proper placement of beds away from direct sunlight and noise sources, adequate storage solutions such as closets and dressers, and sufficient lighting options including natural light through windows or artificial fixtures like ceiling lights and bedside lamps.
