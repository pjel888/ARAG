# PD 1096 Rule VII: Classification of Use/Occupancy and Types of Construction
*An Immersive ALE Review Module*

## The Real-World Scenario (The Hook)
Imagine you are an architect tasked with designing a new residential complex. To ensure compliance, you need to classify the building according to PD 1096 Rule VII for use/occupancy.

## Core Concepts
- **Classification of Use/Occupancy**: Buildings and structures must be classified based on their intended purpose or character.
- **Types of Construction**: General requirements guide planning, design, layout, construction, location/siting, installation, and maintenance of buildings/structures.

## ALE Exam Traps and Common Mistakes
1. **Misclassification**: Failing to correctly identify the use/occupancy of a building can lead to non-compliance with safety standards.
2. **Incomplete Details**: Not providing full details for each subdivision within Groups B-I, leading to incomplete designs or plans.

## Active Recall Question
Which group does PD 1096 classify residential buildings under?

A) Group A  
B) Group C  
C) Group E  

> Answer: A) Group A. Residential dwellings are classified in Group A according to the National Building Code (PD 1096).
