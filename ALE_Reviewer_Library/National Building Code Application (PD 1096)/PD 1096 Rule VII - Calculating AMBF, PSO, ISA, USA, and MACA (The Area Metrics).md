# PD 1096 Rule VII: Calculating AMBF, PSO, ISA, USA, and MACA (The Area Metrics)
*An Immersive ALE Review Module*

## The Real-World Scenario (The Hook)
Imagine you are designing a new residential building in an R-2 zoning area. You need to ensure that your design complies with the Maximum Allowable Percentage of Site Occupancy (PSO), Impervious Surface Area (ISA), and other critical metrics as defined by PD 1096 Rule VII.

## Core Concepts
- **AMBF**: Allowable Maximum Building Footprint - The maximum area on which a building can be constructed.
- **PSO**: Percentage of Site Occupancy - The percentage of the total lot area that may be occupied by buildings and structures.
- **ISA**: Impervious Surface Area - The portion of the site covered with hard surfaces like pavement or concrete, excluding USA.
- **USA**: Unpaved Surface Areas - Open spaces within a lot that are unpaved (softscaped) areas such as grassy patches.
- **MACA**: Maximum Allowable Construction Area - Combined total of PSO and ISA expressed as a percentage of the Total Lot Area.

## Additional Details

### Calculating AMBF

**Allowable Maximum Building Footprint (AMBF)** is defined as the maximum area on which a building or structure can be constructed, expressed as a percentage of the Total Lot Area (TLA). The calculation for AMBF involves several steps and considerations:

1. **Determine PSO**: Calculate the Percentage of Site Occupancy based on land use zoning regulations.
2. **Calculate ISA**: Determine the Maximum Allowable Impervious Surface Area according to the type of building or structure and its location.
3. **Establish MACA**: Compute the Maximum Allowable Construction Area by combining the percentages derived from PSO and ISA, excluding Unpaved Surface Areas (USA).
4. **Adjust for Building Height Limit (BHL)**: Ensure that the AMBF does not exceed the allowable volume as defined by multiplying BHL with AMBF.

The formula to calculate AMBF can be summarized as follows:

\[ \text{AMBF} = (\text{PSO} + \text{ISA}) - \text{USA} \]

Where:
- **PSO** is expressed in percentage of TLA.
- **ISA** is also a percentage of the same Total Lot Area (TLA).
- **USA** represents unpaved surface areas which are subtracted from the combined PSO and ISA to get AMBF.

This formula ensures that while maximizing construction, environmental considerations such as open spaces and permeability requirements are met.
